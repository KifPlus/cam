    ● Max tenure: 25 years. Max age: 65 salaried, 70 self-employed.

    ● Credit card: 5% of total limit.

    ● Prepayment: Max 1% or AED 10,000.

    ● Mortgage reg: 0.125% + AED 250.

    ● DLD transfer: 4% Dubai · 2% Abu Dhabi / Sharjah.
